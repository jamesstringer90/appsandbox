# Sharing fixes and features

Thank you for helping improve this project! If you’ve fixed a bug
or added something useful in your own fork, you’re very welcome
to share it through a pull request. AI-assisted changes are
welcome too. Seeing exactly what you changed and what worked
for you can be a big help.

I treat pull requests as suggestions and examples of possible
solutions. Please keep in mind that I’m unlikely to formally
review or merge them, even when they contain a valid fix or a
great idea. I may use them as a reference while developing my
own implementation.

If your contribution helps shape a change I make, I’ll credit
you and link to your PR in the resulting commit or release
notes. Your help deserves recognition whether or not your
code is merged.

Please include a little context about the problem, what you
changed, and any testing you’ve done.

I appreciate you taking the time to share your work—thank you!
